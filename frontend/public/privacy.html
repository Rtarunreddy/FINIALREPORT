Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 16, 2026

Report Ready is operated by Tarun R in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us", "operator"). This notice explains how digital personal data and files are processed when you access or use the hosted Report Ready service at finialreport.onrender.com. For privacy inquiries, requests, or grievance redressal, email clearpagedocs@gmail.com.

1. Information Processed

  • Account Information: Your email address and a salted password hash when registering directly. If you choose Google Sign-In (when enabled), Google supplies your verified email address and account identifier.
  • Documents & Files: Uploaded source documents (DOCX, PDF), formatted document outputs, file names, file sizes, checksums, and expiry timestamps. Document formatting, styling normalization, and conversion occur entirely on the application server. Report Ready does not use your uploaded or generated documents to train machine learning/AI models or for advertising.
  • Service Records: Processing job logs, quota and usage counters, custom formatting templates, team association records, and any voluntary feedback you submit.
  • Technical & Operational Logs: Hosting and network logs may include client IP addresses, HTTP request timestamps, device/browser user-agent headers, and accessed endpoints.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your information is processed strictly to provide the core services: authenticating accounts, formatting documents, exporting files, managing account quotas, maintaining server security, troubleshooting failures, and responding to support queries. Your data is never rented, sold, or shared for third-party marketing.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

The hosted service relies on the following infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Compute & Application Hosting: Render Services, Inc. (hosts the web service container and runs FastAPI/Uvicorn).
  • Database & Object Storage: Neon Inc. (hosts the managed PostgreSQL database and private S3-compatible object storage buckets in the AWS us-east-2 region).
  • Authentication: Google LLC (only if you choose to authenticate using Google Sign-In).
  • Transactional Email: Resend, Inc. (used for password reset or system transactional emails, if enabled).
  • Telemetry & Monitoring: Error tracking (such as Sentry) is disabled by default and only processes non-identifying runtime exceptions if explicitly configured by the operator.

4. Retention and Deletion Lifecycle

  • Document Files: Uploaded reports and generated output files are subject to an automated 24-hour retention window. Cleanup jobs run periodically; files are deleted upon expiration.
  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You may permanently delete your account and associated data at any time via Account and privacy → Delete account and data. Initiating this action immediately purges your user profile, stored documents, custom templates, and any team workspaces you own from the live database and active storage buckets.
  • System Backups: Automated zero-copy database snapshots and point-in-time recovery archives cycle out within a rolling 24- to 48-hour window, after which deleted records are completely overwritten.

5. Security Controls

All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS), authenticated sessions rely on cryptographically signed HTTP-only secure cookies, and bucket objects are private and accessed via short-lived pre-signed URLs. While industry-standard measures are implemented, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users are advised not to upload documents they do not have the right or authorization to process.

6. Rights of Data Principals & Grievance Redressal

In accordance with applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA):

  • You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete erasure of your personal data.
  • You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time (which may require account closure).
  • You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance Officer: For any grievances or complaints regarding data handling, please contact the operator directly at clearpagedocs@gmail.com. Grievances will be addressed within reasonable operational timeframes. If a grievance remains unresolved, you may have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this notice will reflect the latest revision.
