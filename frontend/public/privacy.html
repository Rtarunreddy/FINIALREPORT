Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 14, 2026

Report Ready is operated by Tarun R in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. This notice explains how information is handled when you use the hosted Report Ready service. For privacy questions or requests, email clearpagedocs@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Account information: your email address and a password hash when you register with a password. If Google sign-in is enabled and you choose it, Google supplies your verified email address and a provider account identifier.
  • Documents: uploaded DOCX/PDF files, generated files, file names, sizes, checksums and expiry dates. Formatting and PDF conversion take place on the service's server; the contents are not used by Report Ready to train AI models or for advertising.
  • Service records: job status and results, usage counts, saved formatting templates, team and invitation records, and feedback you choose to submit.
  • Technical records: hosting and security logs may include IP address, request time, browser or device information, and requested service paths. If the operator configures Sentry error monitoring, diagnostic error data may be sent to Sentry; the app configures it not to send default personal-identifying data.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, format or convert documents, return your files, administer quotas and team features, secure and troubleshoot the service, and respond to support requests. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models by Report Ready.

Service providers

The hosted deployment may use Render for application hosting and PostgreSQL, an operator-configured S3-compatible provider for document storage, Google for sign-in if enabled, Resend for password-recovery email if enabled, and Sentry for error monitoring only if configured. These providers process data needed for their services under their own terms and privacy notices. Report Ready does not offer checkout, subscriptions, or payment processing.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded and generated files expire according to the retention setting shown in the app (normally 24 hours on the hosted Free service). Expiry cleanup runs periodically, so removal may not occur at the exact expiry time. Provider-level backups or object versions, if enabled by the operator, may persist according to that provider's retention schedule.

Account and service records are kept while needed to operate the service. Use Account and privacy → Delete account and data in the app to delete your account, stored files, personal templates, job/usage records and any team workspace you own. The account deletion request removes live application records and stored objects; it cannot immediately erase copies retained in provider backups or logs. If deletion fails, the app reports the failure and you can retry or contact support.

Security and your choices

The hosted service is intended to use HTTPS, signed secure session cookies and access controls. The operator and hosting providers maintain the infrastructure; no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not upload documents unless you have the right to do so. You may contact clearpagedocs@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and legitimate operational or legal requirements.

Applicable privacy rights depend on the circumstances and governing law; publication of this notice is not a representation that the service has been independently certified as compliant. The operator may update this notice and will revise the date above when it changes.
